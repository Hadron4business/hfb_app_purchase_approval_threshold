Purchase Approval Levels

Multi-level approval of purchase orders by amount. Team leader, director, CEO - each level with its own threshold and approvers.

The problem

Odoo can ask a manager to approve purchase orders above one single amount, and any purchase administrator can approve any order. Most companies need a ladder instead: small orders approved by the team leader, bigger ones also by a director, the biggest by the CEO.

What you get

Unlimited levels

Each level has a name, a minimum order amount and its approvers: chosen users and/or any member of a user group.

One after another

Levels approve in order of amount. Or switch to "only the highest level reached" if one signature is enough.

Total or untaxed

Compare thresholds with the total including taxes (default) or with the untaxed amount. Foreign currencies are converted.

Activities for approvers

The approvers of the next level get an activity. One click on Approve, and the next level is notified.

Reject with a reason

Rejected orders go back to draft and the buyer is notified with the reason.

Full history

The Approvals tab of the order shows every level, who approved it and when. Everything is also logged in the chatter.

How to use it

1
Go to Purchase > Configuration > Settings and enable Purchase Approval Levels. Choose the amount to compare (total or untaxed) and the required levels (all or only the highest).
2
Open Purchase > Configuration > Approval Levels and create your levels, e.g. Team leader from 0, Director from 5,000, CEO from 50,000, with their approvers.
3
Confirm a purchase order. If it reaches a level, it goes to To Approve and the approvers get an activity. The Approvals tab shows the steps.
4
The approver opens the order and clicks Approve (or Reject with a reason). When the last level approves, the order is confirmed automatically.

Good to know

  • When the feature is on, the native single-amount "Order Approval" setting is replaced (hidden) and the native "Approve Order" button is hidden.
  • Orders below the lowest level are confirmed directly. Without any level defined, all orders are confirmed directly.
  • A user who is an approver of several consecutive levels approves them with one click.
  • Levels are defined per company; thresholds are in the company currency.
